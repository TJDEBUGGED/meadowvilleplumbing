Who We Are

Meadowvale Plumbing & Mechanical is a Mississauga-based commercial plumbing contractor providing reliable, code-compliant plumbing services to general contractors, property managers, and commercial building owners across the Greater Toronto Area.

We specialize in small to mid-size commercial construction, tenant fit-ups, renovations, and plumbing service work.

As an owner-operated business led by a Red Seal–certified plumber with extensive commercial construction experience, we offer hands-on workmanship, clear communication, and dependable scheduling on every project.
